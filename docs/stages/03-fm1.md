# Stage 3: FM-1 (H -> D, GO, D -> H)

## Goal
Fly FM-1 (RFP pp. 26-27): vertical takeoff at H, transit at >= 30 ft AGL,
land in D, wait for the judge's flag (GO), then take off and land in H.

## Files
- `dbvf/upload_mission.py`: added `waypoint()`, `transit_leg()`, `fly_leg()`.
  `run()` (GM-1) now uses `fly_leg()`.
- `dbvf/fm1.py`: FM-1 run, `offset()` (SITL only), `wait_for_go()`.
- `tests/test_upload_mission.py`: waypoint fields, transit leg shape.
- `tests/test_fm1.py`: `offset()` math.

## Design
- Two MissionRaw legs. PX4 auto-disarms 2 s after landing (COM_DISARM_LAND)
  and a disarm resets the mission, so a new leg is uploaded at D.
- Each leg: TAKEOFF (22) to 10.7 m, WAYPOINT (16) above the target with 2 s hold,
  LAND (21). 10.7 m = 35 ft = 30 ft minimum (p. 23) + margin.
  The hold makes the drone stop before the descent (p. 23).
- H = position at start. D = `--d LAT LON` at the event, or `--sitl-d-north M` in SITL.
- GO = Enter key in the companion terminal. Nothing arms before GO.

## Observations
- Test C: after Hold, the drone moved back a short distance toward H, then held.
  This is PX4 braking overshoot: Hold sets a stop point from the speed
  (`setLoiterItemFromCurrentPositionWithBraking`, mission_block.cpp, release/1.18).
  No failsafe message in the log.
- "Connection to mission computer lost" shows 5 s after the script stops.
  It is a warning only. PX4 has no failsafe action for it (Commander.cpp).
